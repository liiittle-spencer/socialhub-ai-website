Restaurants generate high-frequency customer signals across stores, delivery channels, loyalty apps, campaigns, and payments. When those signals stay separated, teams can see what happened but struggle to decide what to do next.

A customer intelligence layer helps connect those signals into growth workflows:

- Identify lapsed regulars before they disappear.
- Create journeys around favorite products and visit timing.
- Separate promotion-driven orders from loyal repeat behavior.
- Trigger local campaigns based on store-level demand and customer proximity.

The result is a more practical operating system for repeat visits, loyalty, and personalized engagement.
